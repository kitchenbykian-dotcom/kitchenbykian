Questions

Bulk Order FAQs

Do you take bulk orders of frozen rolls in Lahore?

Yes. We supply frozen Cheese Chaska Rolls in bulk for events, gatherings, office parties, restaurants and cafés in Lahore. Message us on WhatsApp with the quantity and date and we'll arrange it.

Are bulk rolls delivered cooked?

No. Bulk orders are delivered frozen and ready to cook, so you can fry them fresh when your guests arrive. Please put them in the freezer as soon as they arrive.

How do I cook the frozen rolls for a party?

Deep-fry straight from frozen on medium heat for 5–7 minutes until golden. No need to thaw.

How long can I keep the rolls before the event?

Up to 1 month in the freezer when kept sealed. Once thawed, please don't refreeze.

How much do the rolls cost?

Pack of 5 for Rs 800 and Pack of 10 for Rs 1,500. For bulk quantities, message us on WhatsApp with the number of rolls and your date. The delivery charge is confirmed on WhatsApp.

Do you deliver bulk orders all over Lahore?

Yes. We deliver all over Lahore, or you can pick up from Islampura. The exact pickup address is shared on WhatsApp when we confirm your order.
