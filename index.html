Frequently Asked

How do I cook the frozen rolls?

Deep-fry straight from frozen on medium heat for 5–7 minutes until golden, or air-fry at 180°C for 10–12 minutes, turning halfway. No need to thaw.

How long do frozen rolls last?

Up to 1 month in the freezer when kept sealed. Once thawed, please don't refreeze.
