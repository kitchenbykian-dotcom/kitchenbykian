Frequently Asked

Are the rolls delivered cooked?

No. Our Cheese Chaska Rolls are delivered frozen and ready to cook. Please put them in the freezer as soon as they arrive. Fried, ready-to-eat rolls are coming soon.

How do I cook the frozen rolls?

Deep-fry straight from frozen on medium heat for 5–7 minutes until golden. No need to thaw.

How long do frozen rolls last?

Up to 1 month in the freezer when kept sealed. Once thawed, please don't refreeze.
